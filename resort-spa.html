Resort & Spa

A premium luxury hospitality website designed to evoke elegance — featuring online booking, automated guest confirmation emails, and a flawlessly responsive experience across every device.

Live Site
// Project Overview

A Luxury Digital Experience

The Resort & Spa website was designed to communicate exclusivity and calm the moment a visitor lands on it. Every layout decision — from the full-screen hero imagery to the whitespace-heavy room listings — was made to mirror the physical luxury experience the brand delivers.

The site includes an integrated booking form that validates guest details and triggers an automated confirmation email immediately after submission, keeping the guest informed and the operation efficient without any manual intervention.

100% Responsive

// Problem & Approach

The Challenge & Solution

The Challenge

Luxury brands live and die by first impressions. The client needed a website that would communicate premium quality instantly — not just in visuals, but in performance and UX. Visitors needed to be able to book rooms and feel confident they were heard, all without the friction of a phone call or email back-and-forth.

The Solution

I paired bold, full-bleed photography with generous whitespace and a restrained colour palette to create an immediately upscale feel. The booking form was engineered with real-time field validation and wired to an email automation service so guests receive a confirmation within seconds of submitting — no manual steps required.

// Functionality

Key Features

Online Booking

Fully validated booking form capturing guest details, check-in/out dates, room preference and special requests.

Auto Confirmation

Automated confirmation email fires immediately after booking submission — guest receives it within seconds.

Immersive Gallery

Full-screen photography sections and a curated gallery showcasing rooms, facilities, and dining experiences.

Fully Responsive

Fluid layouts and adaptive typography ensuring the luxury experience holds up on every screen size.

Services Showcase

Dedicated sections for spa treatments, dining, room types, and amenities with elegant card layouts.

Location & Contact

Embedded map, contact details, and multiple ways for guests to reach the property before arriving.

// Outcomes

Results & Takeaways

The Resort & Spa site launched on Vercel and immediately delivered the elevated first impression the brand needed. The automated booking flow eliminated manual email confirmations entirely, freeing up staff time while improving the guest experience.

  • Live on Vercel with zero downtime since launch
  • Booking confirmation emails delivered in under 5 seconds of form submission
  • Consistent, luxury-grade experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop
  • Accessible, semantic HTML scoring well on Lighthouse audits
  • Reusable email template that the client can update independently
