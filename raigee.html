Raigee Portfolio

// Project Overview

A Portfolio That Works Hard

Raigee is a creative professional who needed a digital home that reflected their personality and work ethic — not a generic template, but something bespoke, animated, and genuinely functional. The site needed to do more than look good: it had to actively convert visitors into clients.

The portfolio was built with a focus on performance and polish. Every interaction — from scroll reveals to hover states — was carefully crafted to feel intentional. The contact form connects directly to the client's inbox via an email API, and social platform links are woven throughout the layout so visitors can explore Raigee's work wherever they share it.

0ms Framework overhead
Auto Email on contact
100% Custom Design
// Problem & Approach

The Challenge & Solution

The Challenge

Raigee's work was scattered across Instagram, Behance, and LinkedIn, with no central hub pulling it together. Potential clients were landing on the Instagram bio and bouncing — there was nowhere to go deeper. The challenge was creating a single destination that consolidated their brand identity and made it trivially easy for clients to reach out.

The Solution

I designed a single-page portfolio with clear sections for work, skills, and contact. Scroll-triggered reveal animations guide the visitor through the story naturally. Social links are prominent throughout, not buried in a footer. The contact form triggers an email to Raigee's inbox the moment a visitor submits — no intermediary steps, no missed leads.

// Functionality

Key Features

Scroll Animations

IntersectionObserver-powered reveal animations that bring sections to life as the visitor scrolls through.

Working Contact Form

Validated contact form that sends the message directly to Raigee's email without any page redirect.

Social Integration

Instagram, Behance, LinkedIn, and Twitter links woven into the layout — not just footer icons.

Work Showcase

Curated portfolio grid highlighting selected projects with hover previews and external links.

Lightweight & Fast

Zero framework dependencies — pure HTML, CSS, and JS keeping the page lean and instantly loadable.

Fully Responsive

Every section reflows gracefully on mobile — crucial for a creative whose audience browses on phones.

// Outcomes

Results & Takeaways

The Raigee portfolio gave the client a professional, conversion-optimised home for their brand. The automated contact form eliminated missed enquiries, and the cohesive design language elevated the perceived quality of their work — even before a potential client clicked through to see it.

  • Custom portfolio delivering a strong brand identity with zero template dependencies
  • Contact form wired directly to client inbox — no leads fall through the cracks
  • Scroll-triggered animations creating an engaging, narrative-driven user experience
  • Social integrations consolidating the client's scattered online presence into one hub
  • Fully responsive — consistent experience for mobile-first creative audiences
  • Fast load times with no framework overhead — pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
